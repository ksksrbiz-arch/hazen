## Generation prompts

### river-hero

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset: wide 16:9 website hero for a Pacific Northwest real estate broker. Create an original realistic editorial landscape photograph of a broad calm Pacific Northwest river, a quiet public waterfront promenade with native grasses and trees along the left foreground, distant evergreen hills across the water, subtle modern low-rise homes far to one side. Warm early-evening light, soft blue sky, natural colors, inviting and credible, fine photographic textures. Wide horizontal composition with uncluttered river and sky in the central area for white website heading overlay. No recognizable landmark, no sail-shaped sculpture, no bridge as the main subject. No text, logos, watermarks, signs, people or property advertising. This is illustrative scenery, not an actual listing.

Architecture believable and varied, no oversized luxury mansions, no identifiable street or recognizable landmark, no visible residents, license plates, text, logos, signs or watermarks. Illustrative neighborhood, not a listing.

Gentle natural morning light, believable materials and proportions, no people, no house number, no text, branding, watermark, signs or recognizable address. Illustrative house, not a property listing.
